System Architecture

The Voice RAG pipeline integrates advanced transcription, vector matching, context reranking, large language model reasoning, and vocal synthesis.

1

Sarvam AI STT

Processes speech files into plain text transcripts.

2

FAISS Vector Search

Matches transcription embeddings against chunks index.

3

Sentence Transformers

Generates high-dimensional semantic embeddings.

4

Cross-Encoder Reranker

Reranks raw FAISS hits using deep similarity scores.

5

Google Gemini

Synthesizes grounded context into direct answers.

6

Sarvam AI TTS

Converts synthetic answer text into vocal responses.
